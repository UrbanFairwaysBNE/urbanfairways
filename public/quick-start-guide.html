Signing Up & Making a Booking

Getting started is easy! Create your account on our booking Hub to manage bookings, track your rounds, and access member perks.

Creating Your Account

  • 1 Visit the Hub or download our app
  • 2 Click "Sign Up" and enter your details (name, email, phone number)
  • 3 Verify your email address to activate your account
  • 4 Add a payment method to your account for seamless booking

Making a Booking

  • 1 Log in to the Hub and tap "Book Now"
  • 2 Select your preferred date and time from the availability calendar
  • 3 Choose your session duration (1-4 hours)
  • 4 Select the number of players in your group
  • 5 Confirm and pay using your saved card or account balance

Tip: Members get discounted hourly rates! Consider joining a membership tier for the best value.

Using the Simulators

Our bays are designed for a seamless, automated experience. When your session starts, everything powers on automatically!

Bay Automation

Each bay is equipped with smart automation that handles the setup for you:

  • 1 Automatic Power On: A few minutes before your session, the bay will turn on automatically — please warm up
  • 2 Software Launch: The simulator software will begin launching shortly before your booking time. Please wait until the welcome window closes, then you can begin your session

You will receive warnings when your session is about to finish, giving you enough time to book extra time if required. Your bay will close the apps and shut down when your session finishes. If someone has booked after you, the bay will remain on and they can take over your session.

Bay Tips

You must place the ball in the hitting zone for the simulators to work. Place your ball in the middle strip, in the front half towards the screen. Please do not stand on the putting turf, and ensure your feet are always on the hitting surface.

Understanding Handicaps in League Play

How Handicaps Work in Our League

When you join the league, your handicap determines how you compete fairly against other players of different skill levels. We use our own custom handicap system designed to keep weekly competition fair across all skill levels.

Your First 6 Rounds (Approx. 3 Weeks)

When a new player is approved by our admin team, they're assigned a Starting Handicap based on their experience level. This starting handicap is locked in for your first 6 rounds — around 3 weeks of league play (2 tournament rounds per week).

Example: If you're assigned a starting handicap of 15, you'll use that exact value for your first 6 rounds — no automatic adjustments during this settling-in period.

After 6 Rounds — Weekly Auto-Adjusted Handicap

Once you've completed 6 rounds, your handicap automatically adjusts every week based on your best 3 scores from your most recent 6 rounds. This rolling system rewards consistency and ensures your handicap reflects your current form.

  • 1 Each week we look at your last 6 completed rounds
  • 2 Your best 3 scores (relative to par) are averaged to set your new handicap
  • 3 The updated handicap is automatically applied to the upcoming week's tournaments

Why This Approach?

Our custom handicap system is designed to keep competition fair for everyone — not just the lowest handicappers:

Fair Competition

Everyone competes based on their actual current form

Recent Form Counts

Only your last 6 rounds are used — old scores roll off
